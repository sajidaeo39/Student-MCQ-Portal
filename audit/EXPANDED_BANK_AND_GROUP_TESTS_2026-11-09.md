# Student MCQ Portal — Expanded Practice Bank and Test Modes

Updated: 2026-11-09

## Test modes now configured

| Mode | Classes represented | Question distribution | Questions | Duration |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| Class-wise Mixed Test | One selected class, 3–5 | 3 English + 3 Urdu + 3 Mathematics | 9 | 5 minutes |
| Class-wise Mixed Test | One selected class, 6–8 | 3 English + 3 Urdu + 3 Mathematics + 3 Science | 12 | 5 minutes |
| Group Mix | Classes 3–5 | One randomly selected question for every class-subject pair (3 classes × 3 subjects) | 9 | 5 minutes |
| Group Mix | Classes 6–8 | One randomly selected question for every class-subject pair (3 classes × 4 subjects) | 12 | 5 minutes |
| Overall Group Mix | Classes 3–8 | One randomly selected question for every class-subject pair (3+3+3+4+4+4 sets) | 21 | 5 minutes |

The three group tests are separate buttons on the class-selection screen. Every question in group tests is tagged with its approved subject; mixed-test questions also retain their subject for the result's subject-wise performance breakdown.

## Question-bank expansion

The practice bank has been expanded from 3 to 5 questions in every approved class-subject set:

- Classes 3–5: English, Urdu, Mathematics only.
- Classes 6–8: English, Urdu, Mathematics, Science only.
- 21 class-subject sets × 5 questions = **105 practice MCQs**.
- 42 additional questions were newly authored for practice (2 per class-subject set).

New questions are randomly selected for subject tests and class-wise mixed tests. This increases variety across repeat attempts. Group tests also select a random question from each relevant class-subject set.

## Provenance and quality note

The 42 added questions are **original supplemental practice questions**, not transcriptions from the 247-page reference PDF. They are written to practise grade-appropriate skills and have been checked for a single intended answer. Their skill tags are provisional learning-skill descriptions; they are **not claimed to be official PECTAA SLO codes**.

The previously transcribed source questions remain in the existing question bank. This expansion does not claim that all 247 PDF pages have been fully re-audited, nor that official grade-specific SLO codes have been independently confirmed. A teacher/content reviewer should still review the new questions before using them for high-stakes assessment.

## Important behaviour

- Timer is a fixed **5-minute total test timer**, not five minutes per question.
- At zero, the test is submitted automatically and unanswered items remain unanswered.
- Student name and school name remain optional.
- No additional subjects have been added.
